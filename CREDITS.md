### 2. In-Game Dynamic Procedural Soundtrack & SFX
* **Engine**: *Project SwiftSouls* Web Audio Synthesizer (`SoundSynth.ts`)
* **Composition & Synthesis**: David Swift & Antigravity AI
* **License**: Project SwiftSouls Dual-Tier License (Evaluation & Commercial EULA)
* **Description**: Real-time multi-oscillator chiptune and synthwave music engine synthesizing unique themes for all 9 regional environments and combat encounters:
  * **Wild Overworld** (`world_map`): "Wanderer of the Void" (D minor / F major, 104 BPM)
  * **Oakhaven Township** (`town_oakhaven`): "Pastoral Hearth" (G major, 92 BPM)
  * **Aetheria Enclave** (`town_aetheria`): "Celestial Starlight" (A minor, 96 BPM)
  * **Ironspire Fortress** (`town_ironspire`): "The Grand Forge March" (E minor, 112 BPM)
  * **Meteor Drop Pod** (`meteor_pod`): "Sub-Orbital Desolation" (B minor, 80 BPM)
  * **Dungeons & Catacombs** (`dungeon_map`, `dungeon_floor2`): "Echoes of the Undead" (C minor, 88 BPM)
  * **Imperial Castle** (`castle_exterior`, `castle_interior`): "Sovereign Keep" (F minor, 108 BPM)
  * **Wild Encounter Battle**: "Clash of Souls" (A minor / D minor, 136 BPM)
  * **Alpha Boss Battle**: "Wrath of Extinction" (C minor, 144 BPM)
  * **Procedural Sound Effects**: 10+ synthesized combat SFX (attacks, criticals, magic spells, level-ups, fanfare).
